Before you scan

  1. Use only a website, API, domain or IP address you own or have explicit permission to assess.
  2. Do not enter passwords, API keys, access tokens or confidential query values.
  3. Treat the output as evidence to review—not proof of a vulnerability or security certification.

Beta data notice

There are no user accounts. A browser-session token separates your saved results from other visitors.

Scan history is stored on temporary service storage and can disappear after a restart, redeploy or idle shutdown. Download any report you need to keep.

Useful feedback

  • Which assessment type you tried.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What was confusing or failed.
  • Your browser and device type.

Never post private targets, credentials, response bodies or confidential scan results in a public issue.

Known boundaries

The free service may take about a minute to wake after being idle. Scan history is not permanent, and the deliberately vulnerable local training lab is unavailable online.

SentinelAI does not currently contain an AI model, exploit targets, guess credentials or sweep ports.
